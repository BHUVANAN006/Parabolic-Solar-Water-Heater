# Parabolic Solar Water Heater Using Copper Pipe

## Project Overview

The **Parabolic Solar Water Heater Using Copper Pipe** is a solar thermal energy system designed to heat water using concentrated solar radiation.

The system uses a **parabolic reflector** to collect and concentrate sunlight onto a **copper absorber pipe** positioned at the focal region. Water flowing through the copper pipe absorbs the concentrated thermal energy and exits at a higher temperature.

The project focuses on developing a simple, low-cost and renewable water-heating system that reduces dependence on conventional electrical or fuel-based heating methods.

---

## Problem Statement

Conventional water heaters consume electrical energy or fuel for heating water.

This project aims to develop a solar-based heating system that:

- Utilizes freely available solar energy
- Reduces electrical energy consumption
- Provides an alternative method for water heating
- Uses a parabolic reflector for concentrated solar heating
- Provides an economical and environmentally friendly solution

---

## Objective

- To design and develop a parabolic solar water heater.
- To concentrate solar radiation onto a copper absorber pipe.
- To transfer the concentrated thermal energy to water.
- To study the temperature rise of water during solar heating.
- To reduce dependence on conventional energy sources.
- To develop a simple and low-cost solar thermal system.

---

## Working Principle

The system works based on the principle of **solar concentration and heat transfer**.

1. Solar radiation falls on the parabolic reflector.
2. The parabolic surface reflects and concentrates the sunlight toward its focal region.
3. A copper pipe is positioned at the focal region.
4. The copper pipe absorbs the concentrated solar thermal energy.
5. Water flows through the heated copper pipe.
6. Heat is transferred from the copper pipe to the water.
7. The heated water is collected at the outlet.
8. The inlet and outlet water temperatures can be measured to evaluate the heating performance.
